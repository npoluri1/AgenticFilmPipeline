## THE LEGEND

"ఋతం అంటే సత్యం. భర అంటే మోసేవాడు. ఋతంభర — సత్యాన్ని మోసేవాడు. వెయ్యి సంవత్సరాల క్రితం, ఒక దేవుడు మన పూర్వీకుడికి ఒక మాట ఇచ్చాడు. మన రక్తంలో ఒక్కడు ఎప్పుడూ సింహాసనం మీద ఉండాలి. లేకపోతే లోకం నశిస్తుంది. ప్రతి తరం ఒక సంరక్షకుడిని ఉత్పత్తి చేసింది — ఒక తరం రెండు ఉత్పత్తి చేసే వరకు. ఇది ఆ ఇద్దరు సోదరుల కథ."

(Rutham is truth. Bhara is carrier. Ruthambhara — the carrier of truth. A thousand years ago, a god gave our ancestor a word. One of our blood must always sit on the throne — or the world will burn. Every generation produced one guardian — until one generation produced two. This is the story of those two brothers.)

## 🎵 THREE MUSICAL THEMES

### Theme 1 — "రక్త రాగం" (RAKTHA RAGAM) — Veera's Theme
- **Instruments:** Mridangam + Sumerian throat chant + Carnatic violin + sub-bass drone
- **Inspired by:** Sooreede (Salaar) × If I Had a Heart (Vikings)
- **Plays:** Only at hero moments. Never as background. Enters like a god walking into a room.

### Theme 2 — "ప్రతిజ్ఞ" (PRATIGNA) — The Oath Theme
- **Instruments:** Female voice (ancient Telugu folk aalapana) + tanpura + war drum
- **Inspired by:** Komuram Bheemudo (RRR) × The Pagan Queen (Vikings S6)
- **Plays:** Flashbacks, the Old Man, ancestral memory, mother scenes

### Theme 3 — "విక్రమ్ పతనం" (VIKRAM'S FALL) — The Brother's Doom
- **Instruments:** Detuned strings, broken tabla, distant azaan-like wail
- **Inspired by:** Toofan (KGF 2) reversed and slowed
- **Plays:** Every time Vikram thinks he's winning — audience clue that he's not

## SEQUENCE 1 — "పాడని పక్షి" (THE BIRD THAT DIDN'T SING)
**Runtime:** 8 నిమిషాలు (8 minutes)

---

**[1.1] BLACK**
6 seconds of complete black. Theatrical silence. Audience starts to wonder if the film has begun.

**SOUND ONLY:** A bird singing — a koel. Soft. Pure. Innocent.
Then — a single gunshot. The bird stops mid-note.

---

**[1.2] EXTREME CLOSE-UP — CHILD'S EYE** (8s)
**Lens:** 85mm spherical, T1.4
A child's eye fills the frame. Wide open. In the reflection of the iris — a village in flames. We see buildings collapsing, fire spreading.
The eye does not blink for 8 full seconds.

**SOUND:** Dead silence. No wind. No fire. Just the void.

---

**[1.3] SLOW DOLLY PULL-BACK** (10s)
**Lens:** 24mm anamorphic
Camera pulls back at a glacial pace — takes 10 seconds to reveal the full frame.
The eye belongs to a 12-year-old boy. Standing in the middle of a village square. Barefoot. His face is smeared with mud and blood that is NOT his. He clutches an old, rust-stained sickle in his right hand.
Behind him: his entire village burns. Every hut. Every tree. Every life.

**SOUND DESIGN:** Distant crackling of fire. The boy's breathing — steady, unnaturally calm. A heartbeat sub-bass, very low.

---

**[1.4] GOD-VIEW CRANE SHOT** (12s)
**Lens:** 24mm anamorphic
Drone spiral ascent. 360 degrees. 50 feet high.
The full scale of the massacre is revealed. At least 200 bodies scattered across the village square. Men. Women. Children. The boy stands in the center of it all — a single living point in a circle of death.

**MUSIC: ప్రతిజ్ఞ (PRATIGNA THEME)** — enters. Single female voice. No words. An ancient Telugu folk lament. Just voice and space.

---

**[1.5] DIRECT TO CAMERA — YOUNG VEERA** (8s)
Medium close-up. The boy looks directly into the camera lens. His eyes are dry. His voice is calm — terrifyingly calm for a 12-year-old.

**YOUNG VEERA** (Telugu, steady)
"నా పేరు వీర. నా వయసు పన్నెండు. ఈ ఊరు నాది. ఇప్పుడు నేను ఒక్కడిని మిగిలాను."
("My name is Veera. I am twelve. This village was mine. Now I am the only one left.")

**Camera holds** for 3 full seconds after he finishes. The stillness is the point.

---

**[1.6] FOOTSTEPS APPROACHING** (10s)
**Lens:** 50mm anamorphic
Low angle. Feet walking through ash and smoke. A heavy wooden staff strikes the ground. The sound is the only thing breaking the silence.
Camera tilts up to reveal **THE OLD MAN** (Mohanlal).
Tall. Lean. A white beard that has never seen a barber. A face that looks carved from old stone. One eye is clouded — blind. The other sees everything.

He stops 5 feet from the boy. Looks down at him. A long pause.

**OLD MAN** (gravelly, in old Telugu)
"నీకు బతకాలి అని ఉందా, కుర్రా?"
("Do you want to live, boy?")

**YOUNG VEERA** (without a flicker of hesitation)
"బతకడం కాదు తాతా. ఈ పని చేసిన వాళ్ళని చంపాలి అని ఉంది."
("Not to live, grandfather. To kill the men who did this.")

The Old Man studies him for a long moment. Then — a small, dangerous smile.

**OLD MAN**
"నువ్వు నాకు దొరికావు, ర. ఇప్పుడు ఈ లోకం నీకు దొరుకుతుంది."
("You have been found by me, son. Now this world will be found by you.")

He extends his hand. The boy does not take it. He doesn't need help standing.

---

**[1.7] WALK THROUGH FIRE** (15s)
**Lens:** 35mm anamorphic — tracking shot
The Old Man walks through the burning village. Young Veera follows — three steps behind. They walk past the bodies. Past the burning huts. Past a child's doll on fire.
The boy does not look down. Not once.
They walk out of the village gates. Into the dark forest beyond.
The village burns behind them.

**SOUND:** The fire fades as they enter the forest. Birds begin to sing again — nature does not care about human tragedy.

**DISSOLVE TO:**

## SEQUENCE 2 — "ది రిచువల్ ఆఫ్ యాషెస్" (THE RITUAL OF ASHES)
**Runtime:** 7 నిమిషాలు (7 minutes)

---

**[2.1] INT. ANCIENT ROCK TEMPLE — NIGHT** (10s)
**Lens:** 24mm anamorphic — extreme wide
A temple carved into a mountain. No cement. No plaster. Just raw rock — chiseled by hand 2,000 years ago.
Torches on the walls. A fire pit in the center.
Twelve old men — CHIEFTAINS from twelve regions — sit in a circle around the fire. Their faces are weathered. Their eyes carry centuries.
Young Veera is brought in. He stands in the center of the circle.

**MUSIC:** ప్రతిజ్ఞ థీమ్ (PRATIGNA THEME) — low drums begin. A tanpura drone underneath.

---

**[2.2] THE OLD MAN SPEAKS** (20s)
The Old Man stands. He pours water from a copper vessel into a stone bowl. The water is mixed with ash from the burnt village.

**OLD MAN** (ceremonial, slow cadence)
"ఐదు వేల సంవత్సరాల ముందు, మహాభారతంలో, కర్ణుడు అగ్నిపుత్రుడిగా పుట్టాడు. తల్లి విడిచిపెట్టింది. తండ్రి తెలియలేదు. బ్రాహ్మణుడు అని అనుకున్నాడు లోకం. కానీ అతను రాజు — రక్తంతో, ధర్మంతో, అగ్నితో."
("Five thousand years ago, in the Mahabharata, Karna was born of fire. His mother abandoned him. His father was unknown. The world called him a low-born. But he was a king — by blood, by dharma, by fire.")
(beat — he looks at Young Veera)
"నీవు రెండవ అగ్నిపుత్రుడివి. నీ ప్రతిజ్ఞ ఒక్కటే — ఋతాన్ని రక్షించడం. సత్యాన్ని మోయడం. రక్తంతో అయినా, రక్తానికి వ్యతిరేకంగా అయినా."
("You are the second Son of Fire. Your oath is one — to protect Rutham. To carry truth. With blood, if necessary. Against blood, if necessary.")

He dips his finger in the ash-water mixture. Draws a vertical line on the boy's forehead. Not vermillion. ASH. The mark of vengeance, not victory.

**YOUNG VEERA**
"ఋతంభర అంటే నా పేరా?"
("Ruthambhara… is that my name?")

**OLD MAN**
"నీ పేరు కాదు, ర. నీ గమ్యం."
("Not your name, son. Your DESTINY.")

---

**[2.3] THE TWELVE CHIEFTAINS** (10s)
**Lens:** 50mm — sequential close-ups
Each of the twelve chieftains stands. One by one. They place their hand on Young Veera's head. Each speaks one word:

**CHIEFTAIN 1:** "శక్తి" (Power)
**CHIEFTAIN 2:** "బుద్ధి" (Wisdom)
**CHIEFTAIN 3:** "ధైర్యం" (Courage)
**CHIEFTAIN 4:** "సహనం" (Patience)
**CHIEFTAIN 5:** "క్షమ" (Forgiveness)
**CHIEFTAIN 6:** "క్రౌర్యం" (Ferocity)
**CHIEFTAIN 7:** "న్యాయం" (Justice)
**CHIEFTAIN 8:** "రహస్యం" (Secrecy)
**CHIEFTAIN 9:** "విధ్వంసం" (Destruction)
**CHIEFTAIN 10:** "సృష్టి" (Creation)
**CHIEFTAIN 11:** "ప్రేమ" (Love)
**CHIEFTAIN 12:** "త్యాగం" (Sacrifice)

Twelve words. Twelve gifts. Twelve burdens.

---

**[2.4] FIRE CEREMONY** (15s)
**Lens:** 35mm anamorphic — circling dolly
Young Veera walks around the fire pit. The flames cast giant shadows on the rock walls. The chieftains chant in an old language. The fire seems to rise with each step he takes.
When he completes the circle, he kneels. The Old Man places a necklace of old tribal iron around his neck — no gold, no jewels. Just iron.

**OLD MAN**
"నీ ఋతంభరత్వం ఇప్పుడు మొదలైంది. నీ మొదటి పరీక్ష — నీ స్వంత సోదరుడు."
("Your Ruthambhara-hood has begun. Your first test — your own brother.")

**CLOSE ON Young Veera's face.** He does not flinch. The fire reflects in his eyes.

**DISSOLVE TO BLACK.**
**TITLE CARD — carved in stone:**

### RUTHAMBHARA
"ज्ञानं सत्यं धारयति"

## SEQUENCE 3 — "అబద్ధంతో పెరిగిన కొడుకు" (THE SON RAISED ON A LIE)
**Runtime:** 6 నిమిషాలు (6 minutes)
**Vikram's introduction**

---

**[3.1] INT. SHANKAR'S MANSION — MUMBAI — DAY** (8s)
**Lens:** 24mm anamorphic — wide
A massive room. Marble floors. Crystal chandeliers. Walls lined with photographs of politicians and film stars.
7-year-old VIKRAM sits alone at a huge dining table. In front of him: a plate of food he is not eating.
He is staring at a photo on the wall — two men, arms around each other. One is the man we will know as SHANKAR (Bobby Deol). The other is a man we don't recognize yet.

**SOUND:** Only a grandfather clock ticking. Old. Heavy. Final.

---

**[3.2] SHANKAR ENTERS** (12s)
**Lens:** 50mm — slow push
Shankar walks in. Handsome. Expensive suit. A smile that doesn't reach his eyes. He kneels beside the boy. His voice is soft, fatherly, poisonous.

**SHANKAR** (Hindi, gentle)
"बेटा, क्या हुआ? खाना क्यों नहीं खा रहे?"
("Son, what happened? Why aren't you eating?")

**YOUNG VIKRAM** (looking at the wall photo)
"Woh kaun hai, papa? Jo aapke saath hai photo mein?"
("Who is that, father? The man in the photo with you?")

Shankar's smile freezes for a fraction of a second. Then it returns — wider, more practiced.

**SHANKAR**
"वो… तुम्हारे असली पिता थे।"
("He… was your real father.")

The boy's eyes widen.

**SHANKAR** (continuing, voice dropping lower)
"उसे मार दिया गया, बेटा। एक आदमी ने। उसका नाम… वीरा। वही आदमी जिसने तुम्हारी माँ को भी मारा। वही आदमी जिसने तुम्हारा घर जलाया।"
("He was murdered, son. By one man. His name… is Veera. The same man who killed your mother. The same man who burned your home.")
(beat — he grips the boy's shoulder)
"एक दिन तुम बड़े होगे। एक दिन तुम उससे मिलोगे। और एक दिन… तुम उसे मारोगे।"
("One day you will grow up. One day you will meet him. And one day… you will kill him.")

The boy nods. His eyes harden. The poison has been injected.

---

**[3.3] SHANKAR ALONE** (8s)
After the boy leaves. Shankar walks to the photo on the wall. He looks at the man in the photo — Vikram's real father.

**SHANKAR** (under his breath, in Hindi)
"तेरी पत्नी ने मुझे माफ नहीं किया। तेरे बेटे ने मुझे कभी जाना नहीं। अब तेरा बेटा… मेरे हाथ का हथियार बनेगा।"
("Your wife never forgave me. Your son never knew me. Now your son… will become the weapon in my hand.")

**CLOSE ON THE PHOTO.** The man in the photo — Vikram's real father. He has the same eyes as Veera.

## SEQUENCE 4 — "అగ్నిపుత్రుడి నిర్మాణం" (THE FORGING OF AGNIPUTRA)
**Runtime:** 8 నిమిషాలు (8 minutes)
**Veera's training montage — ages 12 → 28**

---

**[4.1] KALARIPAYATTU — KERALA — AGE 14** (12s)
Extreme wide. A mud pit surrounded by palm trees. Young Veera (14), skinny but fierce, faces a MASTER.
The master attacks with a wooden sword. Veera blocks. The master breaks his arm.
Veera does not scream. He walks to the side. Resets his own arm. Picks up his sword. Returns to position.

**MASTER** (in Malayalam, impressed)
"ఇవన్ మనుష్యనల్ల. ఇవన్ തീയാണ്."
("This one is not human. This one is fire.")

---

**[4.2] THE SCRIPTURES — AGE 16** (8s)
**Lens:** 85mm — intimate
Oil lamp. Palm-leaf manuscripts. Young Veera reads the Bhagavad Gita, Chapter 2 — the Kshatriya's duty to fight even his own kin. Beside it: Chanakya's Arthashastra — the science of statecraft. Beside that: a map of the seven states of South India.

**YOUNG VEERA** (murmuring to himself, in Sanskrit)
"क्षात्रं क्षत्रस्य क्षत्रियः"
("The warrior's duty is to protect the warrior.")

---

**[4.3] MUMBAI DOCKS — AGE 18** (6s)
**Lens:** 35mm anamorphic — night
Young Veera — now 18, sharp-faced, tall — sits on a container at the Mumbai docks. Midnight. He watches an arms shipment being unloaded. He memorizes every face. Every crate. Every route.

**SOUND:** Ship horns. Distant city hum. His breathing.

---

**[4.4] WAR COUNCIL — AGE 20** (10s)
**Lens:** 24mm wide
Veera (20) sits with the TWELVE CHIEFTAINS. He is the youngest at the table by 40 years.
One chieftain argues against his plan. Veera listens. Then he speaks — three sentences. The chieftain nods and sits down.
Veera is leading. They are following.

**CHIEFTAIN** (to another, whisper)
"వాడికి ఇరవై ఏళ్ళు. వాడి కళ్ళల్లో వెయ్యి ఏళ్ళ అనుభవం ఉంది."
("He is twenty years old. His eyes carry a thousand years of experience.")

---

**[4.5] BLINDFOLDED KNIVES — AGE 22** (5s)
Veera, blindfolded. Five knives in hand. He throws them one after another — all five hit the board. Bullseye. He doesn't check. He turns and walks away before they land.

---

**[4.6] THE MOTHER'S TOUCH — AGE 25** (10s)
**Lens:** 85mm — extreme close-up on hands
A woman's old, wrinkled hands. She paints ash on Veera's forehead.
This is his MOTHER (Revathi). He does not yet know she is his mother. She is the village priestess. That is all he knows.
He looks at her. Something stirs — a recognition he can't name.

**MOTHER** (in Telugu, quietly)
"నీ నుదుట మీద ఉన్నది విభూతి కాదు, ర. అది నీ గతం."
("What is on your forehead is not ash, son. It is your past.")

---

**[4.7] THE SUMMIT — AGE 28** ⚡ (15s)
**Lens:** 50mm anamorphic — epic wide
Veera (28) stands on a cliff at sunrise. The first light of day hits his face. Below him: TWELVE VILLAGES. All lighting their morning fires. Smoke rises from twelve points.
All twelve are HIS.

**MUSIC: రక్త రాగం (RAKTHA RAGAM) — enters for the first time. Low. Building.**
**SOUND:** Wind. A single bird. The theme settles under.

He turns to the camera. FIRST REVEAL of adult PRABHAS as Veera.
The wind takes his hair. His beard is streaked with premature grey. His eyes — calm. Deep. Unshakeable.

**Music cuts abruptly.** Silence.

**DISSOLVE.**

## SEQUENCE 5 — "మొదటి కలయిక" (THE FIRST MEETING — THEY DON'T KNOW)
**Runtime:** 6 నిమిషాలు (6 minutes)

---

**[5.1] INT. MUMBAI WAREHOUSE — NIGHT** (10s)
**Lens:** 35mm anamorphic — wide
A warehouse on the waterfront. A long wooden table. Underworld meeting.
Veera (30) walks in. He wears a simple white cotton shirt. No jewelry. No gold. Nothing that announces power — except the way the room goes silent when he enters.
He sits at one end of the table.
Across from him: SHANKAR, and beside him — VIKRAM (25). Young. Confident. Dressed in expensive black.

---

**[5.2] THE LOOK** (10s)
**Lens:** 85mm — close-up on eyes
Eyes meet. Veera's eyes. Vikram's eyes.
The camera holds on both faces — intercut for 10 seconds.
Theme 2 (ప్రతిజ్ఞ) plays softly — the mother's voice. A distant memory both brothers carry in their blood but cannot name.

**VIKRAM'S POV:** This man… feels familiar. Like a dream I once had.
**VEERA'S POV:** This boy… carries my blood. I know it. But I don't know how.

Neither speaks. Neither breaks the gaze.

---

**[5.3] SHANKAR INTERRUPTS** (8s)
**Lens:** 50mm — three-shot
Shankar laughs. Claps Vikram on the shoulder.

**SHANKAR** (Hindi)
"तुम… वीरा हो?"
("You… are Veera?")

**VEERA** (eyes still on Vikram. In Telugu, translated)
"ఎవరిని అడుగుతున్నారు మీరు? వీరని? లేక నన్నా?"
("Who are you asking? Veera? Or me?")

## SEQUENCE 6 — "పార్వతి" (PARVATHI)
**Runtime:** 8 నిమిషాలు (8 minutes)

---

**[6.1] EXT. EASTERN GHATS — TRIBAL VILLAGE — DUSK** (10s)
**Lens:** 24mm anamorphic — establishing
A village deep in the Eastern Ghats. No electricity. No roads. Torches light the central square. A festival is underway.
Tribal men and women dance in a circle — Lambadi folk dance. Bright mirrors on their dresses. Dust rising from their bare feet.
At the edge of the crowd stands **PARVATHI** (Sai Pallavi). Her hair is wild. Her eyes are fierce. She is the chief's daughter — but she does not stand apart. She is IN the dance.

---

**[6.2] VEERA ARRIVES** (6s)
Veera enters the village on foot. No car. No entourage. Just him, walking in from the forest.
The dance does not stop — but the energy shifts. People notice. They bow their heads.
Only one person does not — Parvathi. She sees him. She keeps dancing. She holds his gaze.

---

**[6.3] THE DANCE — 2 MINUTE SEQUENCE** ⚡ (2 min)
**Lens:** 35mm anamorphic — fluid, handheld
This is the film's BEAUTY sequence. Two minutes of pure visual and musical poetry.
Parvathi dances. The mirrors on her dress catch the firelight. Her feet move faster than the eye can follow. But she is always looking at HIM.
Veera does not move. He stands at the edge. Not clapping. Not smiling. Just watching.
But his eyes — they are softer than we have ever seen them.

**MUSIC:** A tribal folk song. Fast. Rhythmic. Sara sara sara sara…

---

**[6.4] AFTER THE DANCE** (15s)
The dance ends. Crowd disperses. Parvathi walks straight to Veera. Everyone watches. She doesn't care.

**PARVATHI** (Telugu, direct)
"మీరు చూస్తుంటే అందరికీ భయం. నాకు భయం రాలేదు. ఎందుకో తెలుసా?"
